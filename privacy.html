Privacy Policy

Distributed Wisdom · Effective October 1, 2026

The short version

Distributed Wisdom has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device, unless you choose to email us a quote suggestion.

What stays on your device

The app saves three things on your iPhone or iPad so it works the way you left it: the quotes you mark as favorites, whether the daily quote notification is on, and the time you chose for it. This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you delete the app.

Quote suggestions and email

If you use Suggest a Quote, the app opens your own email app with your suggestion filled in; nothing is sent until you press send. When you do, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to review the suggestion and, if needed, reply to you. We do not share them, sell them, or add you to any mailing list. Ask us at the address below and we will delete your emails.

Tips

Tips are optional and happen entirely outside the app, on Ko-fi. Ko-fi and its payment processor (Stripe or PayPal) handle the payment under their own privacy policies. We see only what Ko-fi shows creators, such as the name and message a supporter chooses to leave.

Notifications

If you turn on Daily Quote, the app asks iOS for permission and schedules notifications on your device. No server is involved and no notification data is sent anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party services or SDKs
  • No network requests: all quotes are built into the app (a suggestion email is sent by your own email app, not by Distributed Wisdom)

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing on its own. The only information we ever receive is an email someone chooses to send us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
