Privacy Policy

Distributed Wisdom · Effective September 29, 2026

The short version

Distributed Wisdom does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

What stays on your device

The app saves three things on your iPhone or iPad so it works the way you left it: the quotes you mark as favorites, whether the daily quote notification is on, and the time you chose for it. This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you delete the app.

Notifications

If you turn on Daily Quote, the app asks iOS for permission and schedules notifications on your device. No server is involved and no notification data is sent anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party services or SDKs
  • No network requests: all quotes are built into the app

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
