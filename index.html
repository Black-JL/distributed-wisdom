Distributed Wisdom

Quotes from great thinkers in economics, philosophy, and literature. An iPhone and iPad app by JL Black.

Support

Questions, problems, or a quote you think belongs in the collection? Email distributedwisdom@gmail.com, or use About → Suggest a Quote in the app.

Rights holders: if you believe a quote in the app should not be there, write to the same address and it will be reviewed and removed if warranted.

Support the project

Distributed Wisdom is free, with no ads, and always will be. The quotes belong to their authors and to all of us. Tips at ko-fi.com/distributedwisdom only help cover the cost of developing and maintaining the app: developer fees, tools, and updates. A tip unlocks nothing.

Common questions

How do I get a daily quote? Open About (the ⓘ button) and turn on Daily Quote, then pick a time. Or add the Quote of the Day widget: touch and hold your home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Distributed Wisdom.

Where are my favorites? In the Library (the books button), at the top. They are saved on your device only.

Does the app collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.
